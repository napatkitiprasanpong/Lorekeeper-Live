# Lorekeeper Live — brag video plan

## Input
Project: Lorekeeper Live (current directory)

## One sentence
Lorekeeper Live is a screen-only demo that lets streamers capture a still frame from a shared browser tab or game window and turn it into a searchable lore card — no Twitch account, no AI, no uploads.

## Who it's for / what it does
Streamers and channel editors who want to canonize stream moments as shareable, searchable cards and surface them on an OBS overlay during a stream.

## What sets it apart
Deliberately screen-only: human-written context, no OCR, no AI vision, no chat scraping, nothing uploaded — captures live in browser localStorage on the device.

## Most impressive claim
"Read the screen. Save the moment. Build the channel's lore." Seeded with a real public Flats Overwatch video (~1.77M views).

## Visual hook
A captured gameplay frame sliding from a live screen share into a glowing lore card that pops up on a transparent OBS overlay.

## Real UI to show
Dashboard screen-share viewport, capture queue, editor modal, archive card, transparent overlay card — using the project's real colors (#111116 bg, #f46aa3 pink, #7fe1b3 green, #b49cff purple) and fonts (Barlow Condensed, DM Sans, DM Mono).

## Tone
`default` — punchy, playful, clean.

## Share caption
Capture your stream's best moments as searchable lore cards and put them on your OBS overlay — Lorekeeper Live, screen-only, no Twitch login needed.

## Storyboard (20s, 30fps, landscape 1920x1080)
Scene 1 — Hook (0–3s): "LOREKEEPER LIVE" + tagline; a frame snaps into view.
Scene 2 — Reveal (3–7s): Share a window → screen connects → capture moment → frame lands in queue.
Scene 3 — Highlight (7–12s): Editor modal — title, hero DOOMFIST, tags → save → card in archive.
Scene 4 — Highlight (12–16s): Feature card → transparent overlay card slides up with the snapshot.
Scene 5 — Outro (16–20s): Recap modal + "npm start · localhost:8000" call to action.
